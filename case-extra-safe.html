01

Overview

Extra Safe is a self-custodial messenger with a built-in stablecoin wallet. You chat, call and share files with end-to-end encryption, and when you need to pay someone you send USDT or ETH right in the conversation. No copying wallet addresses, no switching apps.

I've been on the team since November 2024, almost a year before the public launch. I saw the product grow from a private chat with voice and video calls into a messenger built on money rails, and I designed how it looks and explains itself at every step.

02

The challenge

People had to trust one app with two sensitive things at once: their private conversations and their money. And the product kept changing under our feet. Every few months a new layer appeared: calls, file sharing, a wallet, private balances, cross-chain swaps.

  • Explain crypto ideas like self-custody, networks and gas to someone who just wants to send $20 to a friend.
  • Keep one recognisable brand across the app, the main website and the sister products: Norionsoft, UGTP and EXTRA WALLET.
  • Produce a steady stream of visuals for the blog, Knowledge Hub, store listings and campaigns without slowing the team down.

04

Process

01Interview users02Synthesise03Redesign04Ship & measure

We regularly ran interviews with our users, brought the findings together, analysed them and turned them into concrete changes in the app and on the website. Each round fed the next release.

The biggest shift came from this loop. The first versions were a classic secure messenger: chat, voice messages, voice and video calls. As we learned how people actually used it, the product moved toward payments. Today the wallet works on its own too, but its real point is sending crypto to a person the same way you send a message.

07

What I learned

A product that changes this fast needs a system, not one-off pages. The design system is what let us add a wallet, new networks and new markets without redrawing everything.

Trust is a design problem. When money and private messages live in one app, every label, confirmation and empty state either builds trust or breaks it.

Talking to users beats guessing. The move from "secure messenger" to "money in chat" came from listening to how people actually used the product.
