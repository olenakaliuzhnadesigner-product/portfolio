Cases/Extra Safe

Building trust into a messenger with a stablecoin wallet

Extra Safe is an encrypted messenger with calls and a built-in stablecoin wallet, made by Norionsoft OÜ in Estonia. I joined at the idea stage, built the brand, and now lead product design as the sole designer.

01

Context & problem

I joined in November 2024, when Extra Safe was an idea and a rough draft. Features grew fast: calls, file sharing, a wallet, private balances. The team could not tell which parts of the app built trust and which quietly broke it, so there was no clear basis for what to keep, fix or drop.

02

What I did

  • Built the brand from scratch: identity, visual style and a component library for marketing and development.
  • Designed extrasafe.chat, three ecosystem sites and the App Store and Google Play screenshots.
  • Usability study: one of three designers, led by our lead product designer. I interviewed, took notes, synthesised and co-presented.
  • Since 2026, the sole product designer: I turn wallet beta research into new send, balance and onboarding flows.
03

What we found

Each session had a short interview and 7 scripted tasks on the live app. Participants ranged from military personnel to doctors and developers.

Built trust
  • 10 of 12 passed onboarding without doubts
  • No phone number needed felt safe
Broke trust
  • The store page didn't explain why the app is safe
  • 10 couldn't tell Meetings from Calls
  • 4 would quit over disappearing messages
04

What it led to

A direction for the founder: a private messenger that needs no phone number, for people whose privacy is critical. And 5 prioritised proposals:

  1. Disappearing messages become an option; chats stay by default
  2. An App Store page built around how the app protects people
  3. Clearer navigation with a real home screen
  4. Meetings separated from Calls, with scheduled meetings
  5. Short, plain security messages and language choice in onboarding

The founder accepted all five. In my own work, I rebuilt the website and store screenshots around one message: “the first messenger built on money rails”. Today the app has 770K+ downloads and a wallet that sends stablecoins right in a chat.

05

Next time

We tested before defining who the product is for. I would pick one clear audience first and recruit for it.
