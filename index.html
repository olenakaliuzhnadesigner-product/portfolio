Approach

How I work

I’m at my best when a product is still messy — too many ideas, too many constraints, unclear flows. I enjoy turning that complexity into structure teams can build and users can understand.

  1. 01

    Start with the problem

    I don't jump into UI before understanding what we're actually solving, who we're solving it for and what matters to the product.

  2. 02

    Make complexity visible

    I map flows, dependencies, edge cases and competing needs before trying to simplify them.

  3. 03

    Design with the team

    I work closely with product, engineering, content and marketing to make decisions practical, aligned and shippable.

  4. 04

    Build systems, not just screens

    I look for reusable patterns, components and design decisions that make the product more consistent and easier to scale.

  5. 05

    Ship, learn and iterate

    A design isn't finished when the Figma file is finished. I care about what happens after it reaches real users.
