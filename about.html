About

Good design is a tool for solving problems, simplifying complexity, and helping both users and businesses succeed.

I started out focused on UI and grew into a product designer who works on real SaaS and B2B products with tangled flows and business logic. Strong visuals are part of what I do, but most of my time goes into the parts you don't see: research, structure, user flows and the decisions behind each screen.

I'm at my best when a product is complex and the team needs someone to make sense of it. I like working closely with founders, PMs and engineers, and I care about handoff as much as the mockups.

Lately I've been building AI into both products and my own workflow. In 2026 my team won the AI hackathon at Shift Education by Projector. I also mentor UI/UX students at Beetroot Academy and INSCIENCE.
