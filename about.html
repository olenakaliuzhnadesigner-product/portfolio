About

Good design is a tool for solving problems, simplifying complexity, and helping both users and businesses succeed.

Product designer with 4+ years in SaaS, fintech and mobile products. I join teams when a product is complex and still taking shape, and turn it into something people understand and trust: from research and user flows to design systems and developer hand-off.

I have led a team of 5 designers, designed 2 SaaS products and a 5.0 Chrome extension, and worked on a messenger from pre-launch to 770K+ downloads. Mentor and founder of a community for women in design.

Lately I've been building AI into both products and my own workflow. In 2026 my team won the AI hackathon at Shift Education by Projector. I also mentor UI/UX students at Beetroot Academy and INSCIENCE.
