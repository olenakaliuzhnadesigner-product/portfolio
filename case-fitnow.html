About the product

One subscription. 250+ studios.

FitNow is a Ukrainian app with a single subscription to a network of sport and wellness studios in Kyiv. Instead of a membership to one gym, people get yoga today, boxing tomorrow and a pool on Sunday.

Competes withSingle-studio memberships, not one-off visits
AudienceCity residents 22 to 40 who pay for themselves, plus parents choosing activities for kids
StageEarly traction. Live on iOS and Android, first paying users, moving to a new monetization model
Product jobTake a new user from install to the first real studio visit, then build a habit

From the client brief

Rethink the activation funnel, the path from the first app open to the first class booking. Focus on product logic, not visual polish: show value fast, lead the user to a first action, use the free trial class as an activation hook.

01

Location comes first

People pick a studio by route, and the route has several anchors: home, work, a kindergarten. Distance from home alone is not enough.

Led to: saved anchor points
02

Google Maps is the learned way to search

Both segments already look for gyms on a map. FitNow asked them to fill Where and When as separate, sequential fields.

Led to: map as the search entry point
03

The schedule decides

A great studio at the wrong time is a dead end. People filter by free time before they look at anything else.

Led to: sorting by schedule match
04

Nobody wants to start alone

Adults feel safer starting with friends or people from their circle. Kids go where their friends go.

Led to: invite a friend
05

Price has to be obvious

Points hid the real cost. People wanted to see the price of a class and their balance in hryvnias.

Led to: pricing in UAH, bonuses as a reward

Opportunity solution tree

8 ideas, 3 bets

Every hypothesis had to remove one concrete barrier between install and the first booking. We ranked eight with ICE and took three into design and testing.

Hypothesis 01
01

Onboarding that pays back every answer

Five short questions. Each one changes what the user sees on the first screen.

Based on

11 steps from splash to home. Gender and Instagram gave nothing back at the start. By the Kano model, a first session needs only the basics.

We believe

Asking only what improves the first results (who it is for, interests, where, how you travel) and opening a personalized home will lead more people to a first booking.

We'll be right if

Onboarding completion grows and more installs reach a first booking.

Screen 1 · Value first

Multi-subscription in one sentence

The first screen states the offer: one subscription for every class, bonuses and free trainings. A touch on the ring starts the flow, so the first action is play, not a form.

Screen 2 · Who it is for

For me, for my child, or both

Segment B got its own path. Choosing a child adds a name and age range, so kids' sections are filtered by age from the start.

Screen 3 · Interests

Pick a few, see them first

Separate tabs for me and for the child. The picks reorder the home feed instead of disappearing into a profile.

Screen 4 · Where

Current location or your own point

Geolocation is optional. A user can type an address, a metro station or a district, and add home or work later.

Screen 5 · How you travel

Walk, metro or car, and how long

Travel mode and time budget replace an abstract radius. People think in minutes, not kilometers.

Transition · Search

“Finding studios 15 minutes on foot”

A short animated pause echoes the answers back and builds trust in the result.

Result · Home

A feed that already knows you

Filters from onboarding stay visible as chips at the top. Free trainings and bonuses get their own rows, so the activation hook is on screen one.

Try it · Screen 5 logic

Minutes, not kilometers

The same five minutes cover a very different area on foot, by metro or by car. The answer sets the starting radius of the map.

Hypothesis 02 · Result of onboarding
02

The map is the search

Route and schedule become one entry point, sorted around the places that matter to the user.

Based on

Google Maps is the learned search tool for both segments. People choose by route across several addresses and by schedule fit. The app split Where and When into sequential steps.

We believe

If a saved anchor address exists, the map opens with studios sorted by distance from it and by schedule match. Manual Where and When stay for anyone who wants to change them.

We'll be right if

Time to booking goes down and search to booking conversion goes up.

Before · 5 taps to a result
  1. Open Search
  2. Choose What
  3. Choose Where
  4. Choose When
  5. Read the list
After · results on open
  1. Open Map, already sorted by anchor and free time
  2. Pick a studio or change Where and When if needed
Hypothesis 03
03

Bring a friend to the same class

The first visit is easier with someone you know. One link invites a friend to the exact class, and both get bonuses.

Based on

In interviews people said they start and keep training more easily with friends or people from their circle. Kids go where their friends go.

We believe

Inviting a friend to the same class with one link, with a reward for both after a successful invite, will grow first bookings from invited users.

We'll be right if

Invited users grow, they book a first class, and they come back for repeat visits.
