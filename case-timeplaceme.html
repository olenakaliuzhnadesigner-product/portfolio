Cases/TIMEPLACEME, Inc

Leading design for an AI-powered social gifting marketplace

TIMEPLACEME is a social gifting marketplace built around gifting experiences, with an AI-powered assistant that guides people through the gifting journey. I worked as Design Lead, shaping the product experience while mentoring a team of five designers.

01

Context & problem

TIMEPLACEME is a marketplace for surprises: people GIFT tickets they can't use and GET new experiences instead of things. The product was only an idea, five designers had to build it at the same time, and choosing an experience for someone else is much harder than picking a present in a shop.

02

What I did

As Design Lead on the project, I set design priorities with the founder before splitting the work, so five designers moved in one product direction. I made regular design reviews the quality gate for consistency and hand-off, and owned the AI assistant Cris: its conversation flows, UX logic and interaction patterns.

03

Key decisions

  1. Two clear paths on the home screen. GIFT and GET, so people know what the app does from the first tap.
  2. Onboarding that tunes the feed. Interests you love and skip, how far you will go, location and notifications, each one optional.
  3. An assistant that follows the journey. Cris greets people in onboarding and in chat offers the next step: gift a ticket, get something new or learn how it works.
04

Outcome

The app launched on the App Store and Google Play and was presented at Web Summit. There are no public product metrics yet; the founder, who managed my work directly, describes it this way:

“What impressed me most is her calm leadership and ability to navigate uncertainty, which is crucial in the early stages of any startup.”

Ekaterina Tsygankova · Founder, TIMEPLACEME
05

What I learned

With five designers, agreeing on priorities first matters more than any single screen: without a shared direction, five good designers produce five different products.
